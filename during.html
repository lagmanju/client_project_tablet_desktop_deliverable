Stay responsive as the work changes.

Use adaptive project management and responsive fieldwork practices to keep your team, partner, and learning goals in view.

When plans shift

Stay open, accountable, and adaptive.

Every project meets friction: a new constraint, a shift in priorities, an unanswered question, or feedback that changes direction. Return to your shared purpose and communicate early rather than trying to solve uncertainty alone.

Keep your partner informed, test assumptions with the people affected by the work, and treat scope changes as an opportunity to make an intentional decision together.
