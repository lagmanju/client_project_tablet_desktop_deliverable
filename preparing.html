Phase one · prepare

Prepare with curiosity and care.

Build a foundation of social awareness, community context, and shared expectations before your project takes shape.

Before the work begins

Approach the work with ethical humility.

Your identity, lived experience, and assumptions shape how you understand a challenge. Taking time to notice that perspective helps you listen closely, ask better questions, and recognize the knowledge a community partner brings.

Preparation is how teams make space for reciprocal engagement and avoid committing to solutions before understanding the context.

Three places to focus

Build a responsible foundation.

Social and Community Awareness

Examine positionality, learn local history, and consider how power shapes participation.

Expectation and Role Alignment

Clarify learning goals, roles, capacity, and responsibilities before making commitments.

Initial Scoping

Identify needs, stakeholders, constraints, and unanswered questions with your partner.
